# Combat Prototype v1

This is the first playable prototype of the **current** As Above, Roll Below combat model.

It is intentionally not a vertical slice. It exists to validate one question before deeper content production:

> Is it fun to see the enemy's locked dice, roll four dice, commit two to the clash, leave two as potential Spoils, and have the winning margin become HP damage?

## Included

- Player rolls 4d6.
- Enemy rolls first and visibly locks according to a readable Instinct.
- Player commits exactly 2 Fight Dice.
- Remaining 2 dice are potential Spoils.
- Damage = winning total − losing total.
- Ties deal 0 damage.
- Player and enemies use HP bars.
- BUMP once per encounter.
- FLIP once per run.
- Three combat profiles:
  - 2d6 baseline enemy;
  - 3d6/highest-two elite test;
  - simple multi-phase boss test.
- Two experimental Spoils rules:
  - FINAL BLOW;
  - BEST SUCCESS.

## Not included on purpose

- run map;
- shops;
- final item economy;
- final enemy identities;
- conspiracy/occult content;
- production art;
- sound;
- meta-progression;
- final balance.

## Playtest questions

1. Does the enemy rolling first make the round feel like a tactical problem rather than a difficulty check?
2. Is the relationship `margin = damage` immediately understandable?
3. Do you naturally feel tempted to preserve high dice for Spoils?
4. Does choosing a greedier pair feel like a meaningful risk rather than an obviously bad move?
5. Does BUMP/FLIP make you inspect a roll differently without creating menu clutter?
6. Which Spoils rule feels better: FINAL BLOW or BEST SUCCESS?
7. Does 2d6 feel like a normal enemy and 3d6/highest-two feel meaningfully more dangerous?
8. Does the boss phase feel like an enemy using the same dice language as the player?

## Status

Prototype only. Nothing in this directory is production architecture or final visual direction.
