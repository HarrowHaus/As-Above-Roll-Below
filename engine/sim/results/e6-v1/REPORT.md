## Interpretation

- Final-Blow Spoils survives the clean shared-core gate. Raw premium rewards are scarce under safe play, reachable under opportunistic greed, and common only under reckless greed.
- Elite +1 reward-band uplift is doing visible work without changing the underlying raw Spoils distribution.
- Seal-Bearer and Threshold Warden now occupy comparable elite pressure bands instead of Seal-Bearer being accidentally easier than normals.
- The First Door produces authored boss-duration pressure rather than a pure HP sponge.
- Normal-enemy lethality is intentionally low under a perfect arithmetic policy. Human playtesting, not another bot-only nerf pass, should decide whether Floor I normals need more pressure.
- High safe/opportunist clear rates are not direct player-success forecasts: these policies inspect exact arithmetic perfectly and several item/consumable systems are still outside modeled coverage.
