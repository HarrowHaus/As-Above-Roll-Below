# REF-0006 — Kelly-Hopkinsville / Kelly Green Men

**Status:** verified encounter-folklore substrate; exact creature claims remain reported testimony, not established fact.

## Canonical subject
The **Kelly-Hopkinsville encounter**, reported on the night of August 21–22, 1955 near Kelly, Kentucky, outside Hopkinsville.

## Common later names
- Kelly Green Men
- Hopkinsville Goblins
- Kelly-Hopkinsville Goblins

These are later cultural labels, not proof of what witnesses saw.

## Domain
Regional encounter folklore / UFO culture / Kentucky local history.

## Provenance tags
- DOCUMENTED police/public response and later cultural history
- CLAIMED eyewitness encounter
- FOLKLORE / UFO lore around the creatures

## Rights / sensitivity
General historical event and folklore are low-risk factual substrate. Specific modern illustrations, documentaries, books, and character designs remain copyrighted.

---

## What is documented

Local and later reporting consistently place the reported encounter on August 21, 1955 at a farmhouse near Kelly, Kentucky. Members of the household went to law enforcement in Hopkinsville reporting that small unknown figures had repeatedly appeared around the house after an earlier unusual-light/object report.

Contemporary/local accounts describe a substantial law-enforcement response to the family's report. Modern Hopkinsville tourism materials openly treat the `Kelly Green Men` story as part of local culture and list annual **Kelly “Little Green Men” Days**.

The National Archives confirms that U.S. Air Force **Project BLUE BOOK** records covering UFO investigations are preserved and available for research, but the exact relationship of the Kelly case to Blue Book has been inconsistently described in later secondary literature and should not be overstated without the specific case record in hand.

## What is claimed / folklore

Descriptions of the figures, their movement, apparent resistance to gunfire, a nearby craft/object, repeated returns during the night, and extraterrestrial identity all belong to the witness/lore layer.

Later `goblin` and `little green men` language became culturally durable shorthand.

## Common errors to avoid

- Do not state as fact that extraterrestrials attacked the farmhouse.
- Do not treat `green` as a simple uncontested original color description. Later popular culture strongly fixed the `little green men` label, while many retellings of witness descriptions describe dark or silvery/metallic figures with luminous features.
- Do not claim Project BLUE BOOK officially solved the event without the exact archival case documentation supporting that statement.
- Do not copy a modern artist's creature design.

## Visual / behavioral hooks

Recurring later descriptions emphasize:

- small humanoid scale;
- oversized head / ears;
- long arms and claw-like hands;
- luminous eyes;
- strange floating, gliding, or tumbling movement in retellings;
- repeated appearances at doors, windows, roofline, and nearby trees.

These are **reported/lore motifs**, not verified zoology.
