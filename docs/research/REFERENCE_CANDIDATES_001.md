# Holds / exclusions discovered during corpus mining

## Skinwalker — CULTURAL HOLD
The title index contains multiple `skinwalker` episodes, but the term originates in Navajo cultural/religious context and should not become a generic RPG cryptid. Do not use without a much more careful cultural review and a clear reason.

## Mother Horse Eyes — COPYRIGHTED-FICTION HOLD
The corpus contains multiple episodes on the internet-fiction work commonly called *Mother Horse Eyes*. It may inform awareness of internet-horror culture, but its characters/world are not assumed available for commercial game use.

## Disney / modern entertainment characters — IP HOLD
The corpus contains extensive `occult Disney`, Hollywood-symbolism, and film-analysis material. This can inform **how conspiracy culture reads media**, not provide a license to put copyrighted characters into the game.

## Living-person conspiracy accusations — DEFAULT EXCLUSION
Do not turn unsupported allegations about living people into enemies, item flavor, or factual codex material. Historical institutions, declassified documents, folklore, public objects, and original fiction give us more durable material anyway.

# Candidate-selection rule

A famous reference is not automatically a good game reference.

A candidate advances when it has at least two of the following:

1. a strong visual silhouette;
2. a behavior that maps naturally to dice mechanics;
3. a documented detail that creates a deep-cut joke;
4. a meaningful relationship to a Floor motif;
5. enough sourcing to separate fact from lore cleanly;
6. low enough rights/sensitivity burden to justify inclusion.

The finished game should contain substantial ORIGINAL material so this list never becomes a checklist of internet conspiracy greatest hits.
